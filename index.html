About

I build games in Unity and C# and take them all the way to players. Seven years of it, from solo mobile releases to Photon multiplayer at a studio. I also build web platforms, and I use AI tools to move faster while checking everything by hand.

I worked as an Associate Unity Game Developer at Anadae in Kochi, where I built the Photon multiplayer layer for an unreleased title. Before that I shipped my own games as an independent developer. I can build for PC, mobile and web, and I am open to remote and on-site roles.

Path

2019

Started with Unity. I began learning Unity and C# by building small games, which taught me gameplay code, interfaces and how a game comes together. I aimed big from the start, building an open-world racing game called The Revenge, which taught me how large AAA work really is. Most of those learning projects never shipped, but they are where I picked up the fundamentals.

2019 to 2025

Built my own games as Pixelaks. While studying, I shipped Into The Fantasy on Google Play, plus earlier releases that are no longer on the store, and learned Firebase, PlayFab, Unity Ads and in-app purchases by using them in real projects.

2022 to 2025

B.Sc. Computer Science, Pondicherry University, MGGAC. I completed my degree while continuing to build and release games.

Jul 2025 to Feb 2026

Associate Game Developer, Anadae, Kochi. Photon matchmaking, rooms and player sync; gameplay features and UI; game design documents; mobile performance work. The company shut down before its title could ship.

Feb 2026 to now

Building on my own again. I kept developing Adhyora, a Firebase academic management platform that began as a Unity and C# college project, was rebuilt as a web app, and is now live at one college, and built Deck of Deception, a browser card game of bluffing and strategy.
